**Thesis: Culture Identity Vs Assimilation**
The series highlights how in the pursuit of dreams and survival cultural identity can be affected. In regards to Gentefied it leads into a deep introspective view of gentrificaiton, hence the use of the title of the show *Gentefied*.
___
**Production Background**
The producers of *Gentefied* (Linda Yvette Charvez and Marvin Lemus), who took a lot of inspiration from their own experiences as first-generation Chicanos who came from immigrant families. There was also a core focus in integrating both feminists, queer, and Latinx individuals in all facets of production. Providing a deep and through representation that is often seen as accurate to many critics.
___
**Historical And Setting Background**
The story is centered around Boyl Hights, a town located in East Los Angeles that has deep roots in the Latinx community and among others. Its often referred to the intersection of many cultures but was often in high occupation of Latinx immigrant families. Thus leading to the main issue of gentrification and the complexity of its effect on communities. 
___
**Synopsis**
The story of Gentefied (Season 1) is how a small Taco Shop named Mama Fina's ran by Casimiro Morales "Pop" (Joaquin Cosio) along with his nephews and nieces: Ana Morales (Karrie Martin), Chris Morales (Carlos Santos), Erik Morales (Joseph Julian Sofia), and Nayeli Morales (Bianca Melgar) navigate life by chasing their dreams and making ends meet and saving the Taco Shop. Each individual dealing with their own situation due to gentrification and living in a complex cultural world. Casamiro dealing with the death of his wife and keeping the taco shop operating with increasing rent, Chris' failure to be accepted within his cultural identity (his chef dreams), Erik's struggles in being an adult and soon to be father, and Ana's artistic dreams and queer relationship. Throughout *Gentefied* these characters struggled with complex situation that not only test their cultural identity, but lets the audience think in a more multi-dimensional manner when it comes to gentrification.
