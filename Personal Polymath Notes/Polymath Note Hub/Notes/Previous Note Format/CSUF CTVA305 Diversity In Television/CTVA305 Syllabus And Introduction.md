**Diversity In Pop-Culture**
* We often want more representation but not fall into cultural stereotypes.
* Hollywood is "business as usual" to a degree, however, TV has been evolving at a high rate.
* **Narrowcasting:** a focus on appealing to a certain demographics or genre.
* These are caused by demographic shifts across the board, and the content they watch influences in the industry.
___
**Diversity Economics**
* Is focusing on minorities a cash grab?
* Whites still dominate most programs.
* Most minorities experience harassment in the television landscape.
___
**What Is Diversity?**
* A range of perspectives, identities, or cultures
* Can lead to tokenism and stereotypes, corporations, businesses, and other entities have various meanings.
___
**Identity?**
* **Identity Politics:** ethnicity, language, gender, race, etc.
* **Identification:** Used in media studies how the viewer associates with the actor or medium.
___
**Re-presentation**
* Since who and how media is created, we can never have a true raw representation of what we are watching.
* We are a "mirror image" of what we are viewing from the real thing
