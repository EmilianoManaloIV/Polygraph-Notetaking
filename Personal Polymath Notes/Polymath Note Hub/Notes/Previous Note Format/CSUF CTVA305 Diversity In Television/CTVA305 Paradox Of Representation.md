**Virtual Lecture - Paradox Of Representation**

	* Are they utilizing a character that is complex and through.

	* Representation of minorities can improve quantity and quality of minorities.
	* May improve the quality of life of a minority.
* How do we universalize something? How to get something we can "connect" to. "integration" = social differentiation.
* Encoding: information stored in production
* Decoding: information received when the media is watched
* Four Stage Of Communication
	* **Production**
		* Current codes used in standard TV
		* Showrunner vs actors
	* **Circulation**
		* Who receives the message and how its context makes a point.
	* **Consumption**
		* The contextual meaning is taken
	* **Reproduction**
		* Reflection of the meaning that is extracted.
	* **Discourse**
		* How did other external factors serve to influence the meaning
* Denotation: Literal definition
* Connotation: Association with words
* Polysemy: capacity for a sign to have multiple meanings
* Three ways to decode content:
	* Dominant Hegemonic Position: Finding the dominant meaning that the producers where creating.
	* Negotiated position: Acknowledges dominant meaning but as caveats.
	* Oppositional Position: Decoding the message in a specific way.
