Scrum Artifacts  
**Team Information (Team Roles)**  
Detailed information of the team roles and responsibilities.  
**Product Backlog**  
Screenshots of the product backlog, or share the excel sheet of the product backlog and  
information about the product backlog requirements.  
**Sprint Planning**  
Define the work to be done, plan the sprint backlog, goals.  
**Sprint Backlog**  
Screenshots of the sprint backlog, or share the excel sheet of the sprint backlog and  
information about the sprint backlog requirements/ user stores/bugs/tasks assignment  
dashboard, progress dashboard.  
**Scrum Meeting details/minutes**  
List the meeting dates, and minutes of the meeting, attendance/participation.  
**Burndown Chart**  
Burndown chart of the sprints.  
**Sprint Review**  
Sprint review – outcomes, feedback from stakeholders, potential adjustment to the product  
backlog.  
**Sprint Retrospective**  
Inspect how the last sprint went with regard to individuals, interactions, processes, tools,  
and their Definition of Done. Identified improvements to be implemented in the next sprint.
