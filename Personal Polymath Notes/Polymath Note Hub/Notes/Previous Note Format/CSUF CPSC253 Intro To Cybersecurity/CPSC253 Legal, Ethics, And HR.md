**Computer Crime**
* A criminal activity in which computers or computer networks are used as a **tool**, **target**, or a **place** of criminal activity.
___
**Types Of Computer Crime**
* Is it a target? To get data or damage the computer.
* Does it store data? Storing data it shouldn't have.
* Does it communicate with others? Is the network being utilized for criminal activity.

**CCPA**
* Rights for Californians
* Right To Know: What personal data is collected and why
* Right To Delete: Ask business to erase data
* Right to opt-out: Stop businesses from selling data
* Right to non-discrimination: Cannot be treated differently for exercising rights.
___
**CPRA**
* Builds on CCPA
* Adds sensitive personal data race, health, geolocation, biometrics.
* CPPA to enforce the laws
* Stricter methods to maintain that our data is kept private.
___
**COPPA - Protecting Children Online**
* Protects children under 13 years old.
* Has penalties associated with it and be up to millions.
* Collecting a child's name for a contest is OK.
* Tracking kids location without permission is illegal.
___
**GLBA - GRAMM-LEACH-BLILEY ACT**
* Secures financial institution's data on customers.
* There must be a willing effort by the institution to protect data.
