**Strategy For Risk Assessment**
* Follows a systematic approach and follows the PDCA model which ensures improvement over time.
* Depending on the size or the organization, risk assessment may be approached differently.
* PDCA
	* Plan: Establishing what needs to improve and what is the defined outcome
	* Do: Test on a small scale
	* Check: Analyze test results
	* Act: Standardize the change with the larger system
* **Baseline:** Standard security policies across the ecosystem
* **Informal:** Quick checkup of the current security implementation
* **Detailed:** Structured and have deep testing.
* **Combined:** Hybrid approach to the risk assessment.
