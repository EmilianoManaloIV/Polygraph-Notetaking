**Habituation And Sensitization**
* **Habituation:** Occurs when an organism reduces its responses to unchanging, harmless stimuli.
* **Sensitization:** When repeated exposure to strong stimulus increase responses of other stimuli.

**Learning At The Synapse (In Invertebrates)**
* Invertebrates have big neurons which means we can view their synapse and action potentials.
* Gill-withdrawal reflex (Aplysia Releases Waste & Seawater
	* **Short Term Habituation:**
	* Lessons due to reduces activity at the synapse between sensory neurons and motor neurons.
	* A direct result of decreased neurotransmitter release
	* **Short Term Sensitization:**
	* Increased neurotransmitter release by sensory neuron, which causes increased neurotransmitter release in the motor neuron, creating a strong than normal withdrawal.

**Types Of Memory**
* Memory is the recollection of information seen or observed (generally). No boundary between learning and memory (they cycle each other).
* Information processing models (stages of memory from short term to long term)
___
**Atkinson-Shiffrin Model Of Memory**
* 1. **Sensory Input:** Vision, hearing, smell, touch, and balance
* 2. **Sensory Memory:** Large capacity and lasts up to 1-2 seconds.
* 3. **Pay Attention**
* 4. **Short-Term Memory:** Small (5-9 items) capacity & lasts up to 15-18 seconds (requires rehearsal)
* 5. **Long-Term Memory:** Unlimited capacity and lasts indefinitely. (Storage and Retrieval)
___

* **Non-Declarative:** Implicit and unconscious but easy to demonstrate or perform.

	* **Priming:** Exposure to a stimulus changes subsequent responding to another stimulus.
