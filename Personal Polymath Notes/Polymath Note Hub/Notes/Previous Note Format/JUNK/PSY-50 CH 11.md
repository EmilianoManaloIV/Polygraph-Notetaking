**Confounding & Obscuring Variables**
* **One-Group Pretest/Posttest Design:** Testing without using another comparison group.
* **Threats To Internal Validity:**
	* Design Confound
	* Selection Effect
	* Order Effect
	* Maturation: Spontaneous change in behavior over time, often controlled for by comparison group.
	* History: Some external event affects most members of the treatment group at the time of treatment.
	* Regression Of The Mean: Extremely low or high scores at Time 1 are less likely to be extreme at Time 2.
	* Attrition: Some participants drop out of the study from pretest to posttest
	* Testing: A change of *participants* as a result of experiencing the DV more than once.
	* Instrumentation: Occurs when the *measuring instrument* changes over time
	* Observer Bias: Researcher's expectations influence how the interpret results. (Double-Blind Study)
	* Demand Characteristics: Participants figure out the hypothesis and change their behavior in that direction (Masked Study)
	* Placebo Effects: People improve, but only because they believe they are receiving a valid treatment.
	* Selection-History Threat: An outside event of factor systematically affects participants at one level of the IV
	* Selection-Attrition Threat: Participants in one experimental group experience attrition.

**What If The Independent Variable Does Not Make A Difference**
* Not enough variance between groups (Means)
* Too much variance within groups (Distribution)
___
**Not Enough Between Groups Difference**
* Weak manipulation (Not enough to effect anything) *Use A Manipulation Check*
* Insensitive Measures (Dependent Variable Is Not Sensitive) *Use A Refined Background
* Ceiling And Floor Effects (Is the variables not providing enough of a ceiling *Use DV measured that allow variability, use a manipulation check for IV*
___
**Between Groups Difference Vs Within Groups**
* Too much variability equates to obscure the difference between means.
___
**Too Much Variability Within-Groups**
* Measurement Error (There is error within the variable) *Use a precise measure, measure more often*
* Individual Differences (There is too much differences) *Measure more people, use a matched design, use a repeated measures design*
* Situation Noise (Environment influences the data) *Use a controlled environment*
