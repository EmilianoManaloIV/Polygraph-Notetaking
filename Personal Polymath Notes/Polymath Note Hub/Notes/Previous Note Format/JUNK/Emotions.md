**Emotions**
* Emotions are universal
* Always used in **nonverbal communication**.
* Generally use the whole body to express an emotion
* Emotions evolved in-order to survive (care and apprehension)
___
**What Are Emotions?**
* Two components
	* **Physical Sensation:** Rapid heartbeat, butterflies, sweaty palms, etc.
	* **Conscious, Subjective Experience Of Feeling:** feeling anxious, fear, or excitement
* $\text{Fell Down The Stairs} \to \text{Pain Fear} \to \text{Anxiety Over Stairs}$
* **Valance:** general positive or negative quality
* **Innervation** of the nerves into the face regulates the facial expression of emotion we pay attention to the face, esp. the eyes in reading another person's emotion.
* They are universal across countries.
___
**The Expression Of The Environment**
* **Environmental Influences:** seems to be innate, genetically determined emotional responses that immediately come under the influence of social and environmental feedback (like culture and learning).
* Presence of others influences *intensity* of emotional expression.
* Cultures generally have different *display rules*, or norms that define when and where you can express emotion.
* **Individual Differences:** Our overall intensity and ability to identity other' emotions correctly differs quite a bit between people.
* The ability to *interpret* emotional expressions of others is heavily influenced by genetics.
___
**Theories Of Emotion**
* **James-Lange Theory:** A sequence of events result in an emotional experience. An awareness of our physiological state leads to the identification (or labeling) of a subjective feeling. **Facial Feedback Hypothesis:** our facial expressions affect the way that we feel.
* **Cannon-Bard Theory:** Physical responses and subjective responses occur simultaneously and independently. 
* **Schachter-Singer Theory:** A stimulus elicit during feeling
___
**The Biology Of Emotion**
* **ANS:** flight of right responses, the **PNS** for rest and recovery
* **Amygdala:** Regulation of emotions and arousal
* **Cingulate Cortex:** Gateway between the amygdala, other limbic structures and the frontal lobes of the cortex.
* **The anterior SC (ACC):** processes feelings associated with physical and social pain and feelings of being socially excluded. Cooperated with the orbitofrontal cortex in processing reward and decision making and in the inhibition of aggression.
* **The Basal Ganglia:** Coordination of voluntary movement in responses to emotional stimuli.
* **Cerebral Cortex:** Frontal lobe damage produces emotional disturbance. Damage in the frontal lobes is associated with difficult making and following through with plans. It is also associated with reduction in emotional feelings, especially those of fear and anxiety.
	* **Right hemisphere** plays a stronger roll in negative emotions, emotional aspects of language.
	* **Left Hemisphere** play a stronger roll in positive emotions, meaningfulness of language.
	* **Dichotic Listening Task:** where different information is presented to each ear, can be used to asses the functioning of the two hemispheres.
	* Asymmetry in the intensity of facial expressions. (2/3 Of The Face)
* **Frontal Lobotomies:** were performed and caused significant emotional disturbance.
