___
*How To Get Good Samples: Randomization*
___
**Populations And Sample**
* **Population:** The whole
* **Sample:** A part of the whole
* **Census:** Everybody is included to collect data
* *What is our population of interest?
	* Biased sample technique, not likely representative of everyone else in the population.
	* Representative Sampling Technique, randomly chosen and has an equal chance of selection.
	* **Probability Sampling:** Every member of the populations has an equal chance of being selected.
___
**Obtaining A Representative Sample: More Probability Sampling Techniques**
* **Simple random sample.**
* **Cluster sample, multistage sample:** Groups of people are randomly selected (churches, nursing homes, etc.), groups of people are randomly selected, then members of that group are randomly selected.
* **Stratified Random Sample:** Percentage of the whole block, making the sample ratio the same as the population categorization. 
* **Systematic Sample:** "Dice Roll", select a person based on a random number.
___
**Random Sampling Vs Random Assignment**
* **Random Sampling:** Increased external validity 
* **Random Assignment:** Used only in experimental designs to assign participants to groups at random. (OFTEN IN EXPERIMENTS)
	* INCREASES INTERNAL VALIDITY
___
**Settling For An Unrepresentative Sample: Biased Sampling Techniques**
* Convenience sample: Contact those who are easy to have contact with
* Purposive Sample: You find a certain sample you want to use for your data
* Self-selected Sample: People those who invite themselves as a sample (extremely bias, politics)
* Snowball Sampling: Small group or niche population, networking from person to person (Pyramid Scheme).
___
**Interrogating External Validity: What Matters Most?**
* When a representative sample is not a top priority
	* Nonrandom samples in the real world (shoe size)
	* Nonrandom samples in research studies (divorce)
	* Is there a big difference when sampling the population
___
**Interrogating External Validity: What Matters Most?**
* Large samples can be more accurate, however, for an association research, its more important to have a representative group then it is to have a large sample size.
