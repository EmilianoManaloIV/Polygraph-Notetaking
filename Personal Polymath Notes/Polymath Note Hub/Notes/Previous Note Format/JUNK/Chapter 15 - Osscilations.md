**Vertical Mass-Spring System**
$$F=kx=mg$$
**Kinetic Energy**
$$\omega^2=\frac{k}{m}$$
$$k=\frac{1}{2}mv^2=\frac{1}{2}m(\omega^2A^2\sin^2(\omega t+\phi))$$
$$E=k+u=\frac{1}{2}kA^2(\sin^2(\omega t+\phi)+\cos^2(\omega t+\phi))=\frac{1}{2}kA^2$$
**Potential Energy Between 2 atoms in molecule, Lennard-Jones potential energy functions**
$$u(x)=4e\left( \left( \frac{\sigma}{x} \right)^\text{12}-\left( \frac{\sigma}{x}^6 \right) \right)$$
$$e,\sigma \to\text{Determined Experimentally}$$
**SHM**

**Simple Pendulum (String And Uniform Density Object**
$$\text{Torque} = -mgL\sin(\theta)$$
$$T=2\pi \sqrt{ \frac{g}{L}}$$
**Physical Pendulum (Object Itself, Non-Uniform Density Object**
$$\text{Torgue}=mgd\sin(\theta)$$
$$I\frac{d^2\theta}{dt^2}=-mgd\sin \theta$$
$$\sin(\theta) \approx \theta$$
$$\frac{d^2\theta}{dt^2}=-mgd \theta/\theta$$
$$T=2\pi \sqrt{\frac{I}{mgd}}$$
**Torsion Pendulum (Rotating Disk)**
$$\frac{Id^2\theta}{dt^2}=-\kappa \theta$$
$$\frac{d^2\theta}{dt^2}=-\frac{\kappa \theta}{I}$$
$$T=s\pi \sqrt{ \frac{T}{k} }$$
**Damped Harmonic Motion**

$$F_{\text{damp}}=-bv=-\frac{bdx}{dt}$$
$$\frac{md^2x}{dt^2}=-kx-\frac{bdx}{dt}$$
$$x(t)=Ae^\left( -\frac{bt}{2m} \right)\cos(\omega t+\phi)$$
$$\omega=\sqrt{ \frac{k}{m}-\left( \frac{b}{2m} \right)^2 }$$
$$\omega =o \to b=\sqrt{ 4mk }$$
$$b<\sqrt{ 4mk }\to\text{underdamped}$$
$$b=\sqrt{ 4mk }\to \text{crticially damped}$$
$$b>\sqrt{ 4mk }\to\text{overdamped}$$
**Forced Oscillations**
$$F=F_{\text{ext}}\cos(\omega t)$$
$$\frac{md^2x}{dt^2}=-kx-\frac{bdx}{dt}+F_{\text{ext}}\cos(\omega t)$$
$$x(t)=A\cos(\omega t-\phi)$$
$$A=\frac{\frac{F_{\frac{\text{ext}}{}}}{m}}{\sqrt{ (\omega ^2-\omega t^2)+\left( \frac{b\omega}{m} \right)^2 }}$$
**Resonance**: Large oscillations with small $F_{\text{ext}}$
