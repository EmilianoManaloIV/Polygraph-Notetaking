___
**Learning**
* The adaptation to the environment in order to survive.
	* Reflexes: Involuntary responses to stimuli
	* Instincts: Complex behaviors (Similar to reflexes)
	* Learning: relatively permanent change change in behavior due to experience.
___
**Reflexes**
* Fast and reliable responses
___
**Instincts**
* Proceed in a predictable stereotypical behavior
___
**Learning**
* Learning is the plastic way of learning a certain behavior to adapt to the environment.
