**Dreaming During REM and NREM**
* Dreams occur during  both REM and NREM
* More likely in REM sleep
* Lengthy, complicated, vivid, and story-like
* NREM are logical
* Most dreams occur in familiar places and involve routine activities
* 70% of dreams have negative content
___
**What Are Common Dream Themes**
* Being chase
* Falling
* Flying
* Appearing Naked
* Being unable to find a restroom
* Being frozen with fright
* Taking exams, one is unprepared for
* Male's report more aggressive dreams
* Viewing upsetting information enforces likelihood of negative dreams.
___
**Why Do We Sleep?**
* Sleeping keeps us safe (Don't have to worry about predators).
* Sleep heals our body and helps with retaining energy.
* **Neurogenesis:** Creation and connections of neurons
* **Growth Hormones:** Released during sleep stages 3 & 4, aids in creation of muscle, bone mass, and maintain immune system function.
* Consolidates the use of short-form information
* NREM sleep often leads to REBOUND.
	* Increased physical demands correlates to increased amount of sleep
* Benefits Of REM Sleep
	* Increases after learning something
	* Plays a role in brain development
	* REM rebound (natural "make-up" sleep)
	* Deprivation causes irritability and unable to focus![[Ch 11 Sleep Disorders.docx]]

**The Initiation Of Wakefulness & Sleep**
* Neurochemicals involved in sleep and wakefulness
	* **Ventrolateral Preoptic Nuclear Of The Hypothalamus (VLPO)** "Inhibitory"
		* Raphe Nucleus: Major Seratogenic pathway (Reticular Formation): Often inhibited by GABA to promote sleep. Influences both excitatory and inhibitory brain function. 
			* Increased activity during waking hours and slow amount of activity during sleep hours.
		* Locus Coeruleus (Pons): Major noradrenergic (Norepinephrine Depending On Receptor Site) 
			* Arousal and wakefulness
			* Burst of energy
			* Receives inhibitory (GABA from VLPO) to promote sleep
			* Activity is low during slow wave sleep and almost zero during REM sleep.
		* Basal Forebrain (Basal Forebrain) "excitatory" associated with beta activity and REM sleep
			* Is also projects to the cortex to facility waking up
			* Forebrain activity is increased during REM and low during slow wave sleep.
