**Review Of Three Causal Criteria**
* Covariance: Is there a relationship?
* Temporal Precedence: A before B? (Cross-lag panel designs)
* Internal Validity: No other confounds (multiple regression)
___
**Longitudinal Length**
* Study the same variables at different times
___
**Longitudinal Designs**
* Cross-Sectional Correlations: Two variables measured at different times are correlated?
* Autocorrelations: Is the same variable from one time correlated to another?
* Cross-Lag Correlations: Taking one variable at one time correlated with another variable at a different time.
	* One or the other, or all related amongst each other
