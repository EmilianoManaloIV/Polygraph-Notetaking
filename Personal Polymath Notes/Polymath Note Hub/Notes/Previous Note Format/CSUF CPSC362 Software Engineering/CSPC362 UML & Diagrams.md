**Overview Of Use-Case Diagram**
* **Actor (Stick Figure):** hardware, third party software, user, external user, entity
	* Human User
	* Hardware Device
	* Another system
	* **Passive:** Not involved with direct functionality
	* **Active:** Directly involved with the functionality
* **Use Case (Oval):** Functionality 
* **System Boundary (Rectangle):** The system as a whole
___
**Use Case Definition**
* How is the end-user interacting with the system
	* Who is the primary actor
	* What are the actor's goals
	* What precondition should exist
	* What main tasks or functions are performed by the actor
	* What extensions might be considered
	* Is there multiple decisions for the actor
	* What system information will change
	* What information will the actor acquire
	* What information does the actor want from the system
	* Does the actor wish to be informed about unexpected changes.
