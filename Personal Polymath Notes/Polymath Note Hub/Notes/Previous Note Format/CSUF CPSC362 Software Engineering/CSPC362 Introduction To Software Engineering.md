___
**Outline**
* What are the attributes of good quality software?
* **Software:** A function or application that does something to achieve a certain output.
* **Engineering:** A systemic approach based on structure and execution of a product.
* **Software Engineering:** The job of organizing and maintaining the software for future and continues use.
___
**Importance Of Software Engineering**
* Many entities relies on software to complete a certain set of goals.
* Software engineering methodologies often equate to cheaper development cost.
___
**Software Crises**
* Software can be stagnant due to technological and hardware improvements over the years.
* Software doesn't follow the requirements nor fulfills the proper quality as tested.
* Software may balloon out of scope that is not realistic.
* Research and development may be way too intensive
___
**Complexity**
* The more robust the software, the more time and resources is required for execution.
___
**Software Cost**
* Development cost is less than ownership cost.
* Scalability is a **cost** based methodology.
* Errors early on aren't so bad, however, when down the road it may determinantal to the whole system.
	* This is why we have testers
___
**Software Engineering Problem Solving**
* Formulate, plan, compare solutions, decide on best case of scenario, planning, and execution of idea.
* Cost analysis, implementation analysis, what makes sense and fulfills the requirements.
___
**Modeling Activity**
* Software is evolving and requirements do change over time.
* **Interdependency:** the developers create the software, you compile them together to make a product.
___
**Software Complexity**
* Break down each category and tree the system.
___
**Essential Attributes Of Good Software**
* Maintainability "easy to keep"
* Dependability And Security "easy to secure"
* Efficiency "easy to keep creating"
* Acceptability "easy to interact with and is responsive"
* Cybersecurity is extremely important
___
**Requirements Engineering Is Important**
* Confusion and misinterpretation of user requirements can exponentially spin out of control.
___
**Software Development Lifecycle**
* Requirements and engineering analysis, Design (UML), Implementation (source code), testing and validation, software maintenance
