___
**What Are Projects?**
* They are often complex and have a-lot of moving parts. These things need to be considered in order to get the **big picture**.
___
**Align People And Objectives**
* Communicate with your **sponsor** to facilitate proper understanding of vision, goals, and risks. You are also both in agreement of the risks and how to overcome them.
* Create a **Project Charter** that gives a simple and easily understandable way for others to understand the document.
	* **Objectives**
	* **Scope**
	* **Schedule**
	* **Risks**
___
**Know Your Stakeholders**
* **Stakeholders** can be product owners, end-users, etc.
* Use an **Empathy Map** to relate to the stakeholder even further to understand their wants and needs.
___
**Project Requirements**
* Always bounce between stated requirements and requirements that may change due to stakeholder feedback.
* Always understand the scope and effort of the feedback to implement the requirement.
* Utilize
	* One-on-one interviews
	* Surveys
	* Shadowing Or Observation
___
**Break Down The Work**
* **Scope Creep*** occurs when a requirement suddenly creates other requirements not originally planned.
* Make sure to collaborate with your team and gauge the requirements based on how critical the requirement is.
* Utilize a **Work Breakdown Structure** to abstract a requirement to its fundamental components.
	* Research
	* Design
	* Develop
	* Test
___
**Sequence The Work**
* You need to manage your time well with a **schedule** and organize that time based on **dependencies** in order to get features shipped in a timely and organized matter.
* Dependency Methods:
	* Finish-To-Start: The prior task must be completed until starting the next task related it to it.
	* Start-To-Start: Start on one task leads to start of another task.
	* Finish-To-Finish: The prior task must be completed in order to consider the related ask complete.
	* Start-To-Finish: The completion of another task is based on the status of the prior task
* Utilize a **Project Schedule Network Diagram** to organize dependency on certain tasks.
___
**Determine Who Does The Work**
* You need to identify the strong suits of your team in order to leverage what tasks should be delegated.
* Base a-lot of delegation based on the risk and skill level of your team. 
* Utilize a **talent and skills spreadsheet** to determine the skillset and experience of your team
___
**Creating The Project Schedule**
* Schedule the time allotted for each task and should be available to your team and stakeholders. Should be universally agreed that this indeed is the time you need.
* Define **milestones** which critically represent the progress of your project and define which tasks are associated with who.
___
**Knowing Your Risks And Create Mitigation Plans**
* You need to weight the risks and opportunities to determine an accurate anticipation of what countermeasures need to be deployed.
* Risk Categorization
	* Avoidance
	* Mitigation
	* Transferal
	* Acceptance
* Utilize a **Risk Register** to gauge what could be improved to lower risk.
___
**Review The Plans With Your Sponsors**
* Make sure sponsors and stakeholders thoroughly view and analyze the plan.
