**Introduction To Scrum**
* Often used on product development and deals with higher amounts of chaos. Its an attempt to **facilitate that chaos** into something incredibly **adaptable** with the use of **feedback loop**.
* A **Sprint** is something that involves planning, testing, and every element of the **waterfall method**.
* **Roles Of Scrum**
	* **Product Owner:** Focuses on **R.O.I.**, final arbiter of the requirements (final call) and often makes the final decision.
	* **Scrum Development Team:** Cross-Functional, creates a shippable product, collaborates with each other's specialty, its self-organizing
	* **Scrum Master:** Has no management authority and not a manager, teach people, promotes engineering practices, facilitates the scrum methodology.
* **Artifacts**
	* **Product Backlog:** Things we want to do to create the product. Often ranked from highest to lowest priority.
	* **Sprint Backlog:** The backlog for the current working sprint that is often in phases.
* **Meeting**
	* **Sprint Planning Meeting:** Product owner and team choose items from the backlog. Then add that item into the sprint backlog.
	* **Daily Scrum Meeting:** Belongs to the team and not the scrum master or product owner.
	* **Sprint Review Meeting:** Provides a product to stakeholders and customers and get feedback and input. 
	* **Retrospective Meeting:** Focuses on how we operate and focuses on what we do well and what can be improved.
	* **Backlog Refinement Meeting (Grooming):** Team and product owner looks at the backlogs and turns **Epics (Large Backlog Items)** into **User Stories (Components Of The Epics)** into smaller doable components in the backlog.
___
**Backlog Refinement Meeting**
* Who Is Present: Product Owner, Scrum Development Team, Scrum Master
* Scrum master facilitates and demonstrates what PBI should be focused on and if its something we break down into a smaller PBI's in order to be done in a sprint.
* Estimation Of Effort
* Clarification Or Requirements
* Breaking down of larger PBIs
___
**Sprint Planning Meeting**
* Scrum master facilitates what PBI from the product backlog will be included in the current sprint.
* From that the **PBI** requires sprint tasks that may be used for the current sprint and is discussed with the Scrum Development Team. Then tasks are then agreed upon and given to those who want them in the development team.
___
**Daily Scrum Meeting**
* What did I already do, what will I do, what's impeding my tasks.
* Adds sidebars when needed to be discussed afterwards.
___
**Sprint Review Meeting**
* Has stakeholders and external sources to give input, SCRUM master gets more PBI's from feedback given. Product owner determines what PBI's are completed or need to given back to the product backlog.
___
**Sprint Retrospective Meeting**
* **Safety Check:** Check if everyone is psychologically safe in the environment.
* **Classic Scrum Retrospective:** what went well and what went wrong **Pluses And Deltas**, how can we improve our process.
* **Focused Conversation Principles:** Objective questions, reflective questions, interpretive question, decision questions.
* **Silent Writing:** Communicate via post it notes.
* **Timeline Retrospective:** A way for members to discuss events in a sprint timeline.
